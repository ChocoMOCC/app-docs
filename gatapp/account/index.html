Identity

Account & roles

Sign in

Open Sign In in the header, enter the email address and NIM registered in Central Auth, then submit. The server restores an authenticated session on later page loads until the session expires or you sign out.

Switch the active role

A user may have multiple assigned roles. Open the role control, select another assigned role, and the portal immediately refreshes application access for that session.

AdministratorAll applications and System Settings.
InternPublic apps plus apps assigned to Intern.
StudentPublic apps plus apps assigned to Student.
LecturerPublic apps plus apps assigned to Lecturer.
Role assignment versus active role

Assignments define which roles you may choose. The active role is the one currently used for access checks. You cannot switch to an unassigned role.
