Operations

ToolPurpose
HealthCheck one or all application endpoints, status messages, and latency. Automated checks use the authenticated /api/cron/health endpoint.
AnnouncementsPublish or schedule Information, Warning, or Critical messages for Public or selected roles.
AnalyticsReview total launches, active users, tracked apps, and most-used applications for the last 30 days.
AuditFilter administrative and security events by time, actor, or action; expand changed-field details.

Timestamps are displayed in the viewer's local time. Audit results show 10 events per page.
