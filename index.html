Start here

Getting started

Socmed Apps brings publishing records, schedules, task ownership, meetings, and performance reporting into one workspace.

1

Sign in

Open the workspace with your assigned account. Your role determines the available views and actions.

2

Check your work

Review overdue, due-today, and upcoming assignments in My Work.

3

Update records

Add publishing details and measured results through Dashboard or Content.

4

Review results

Use Analytics to compare platforms, creators, and content categories.

Good practice

Use the same content ID from planning through publication. Consistent IDs prevent duplicates and simplify follow-up.

02

Workspace basics

Navigation

The desktop sidebar provides access to each permitted workspace. On mobile, use the bottom navigation and More menu.

03

At a glance

Dashboard

The Dashboard summarizes performance and operational activity for the selected date range.

Performance cards

Review total views, published content, reach, engagement, and average engagement rate. Change the global date range to compare reporting periods.

Growth Performance

Switch between Followers, Engagement, and Reach. Each line represents Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube. Followers are cumulative; engagement and reach show monthly totals.

Reading the chart

A flat line may be valid when the selected period has no recorded metric. If axes and month labels are missing too, refresh and check your connection.

04

Personal queue

My Work

My Work filters the task schedule to items assigned to your account. Start with overdue work, then due-today work, followed by upcoming tasks.

  • Open a task to see its date, category, owner, and status.
  • Keep status current so dashboard counts remain accurate.
  • Use Calendar when you need the wider publishing context.
05

Plan delivery

Calendar & tasks

The Calendar organizes scheduled content by date. Task List provides a structured, searchable view of the same operational work.

Create a useful schedule entry

  1. Choose the intended publishing or due date.
  2. Enter a clear title and content category.
  3. Assign the responsible team member.
  4. Add platform and status details.
  5. Save and confirm the entry appears on the correct date.
06

Maintain data

Content records

A content record connects the published asset with its owner, platform, category, URL, and measured results.

FieldGuidance
IDUse a unique, stable content identifier.
DateUse the actual publication date.
TitleUse a short name recognizable to the team.
PICSelect the responsible team member.
PlatformSelect the configured publishing channel.
MetricsEnter numeric values without descriptive text.
URLLink directly to the published asset.

Engagement is calculated from likes, comments, follows, reposts, and shares. Review imported values before saving.

07

Capture decisions

Meetings

Use Meetings for agendas, recaps, decisions, and action items. Keep notes concise and identify an owner whenever follow-up is required.

Recommended recap

Decision: Publish the campaign teaser on 18 August. · Owner: Content Team · Follow-up: Final approval by 16 August.

08

Understand results

Analytics

Analytics turns saved records into trend, platform, creator, and category comparisons.

  • Set the date range before drawing conclusions.
  • Compare similar content and reporting periods.
  • Use reach and engagement together; views alone do not show response.
  • Check the source record when a value looks unusual.

Common metrics

ViewsTotal recorded content views.
ReachPlatform-reported accounts reached.
EngagementCombined recorded audience actions.
Engagement rateEngagement relative to the configured calculation.
09

Access control

Roles & permissions

Your role controls available views and create, update, or delete actions. Restricted controls may be hidden or locked.

RoleTypical access
ViewerRead permitted dashboards and reports.
ContributorMaintain assigned operational and content records.
AdministratorManage settings, access, and broader workspace data.
Need more access?

Contact the workspace administrator. Never share an account to work around a permission restriction.

10

Work outside the app

Data export

Use Export to download content performance data as an Excel workbook. Treat exports as internal operational data.

  1. Confirm the current date filter.
  2. Select Export from the top bar.
  3. Wait for download confirmation.
  4. Store the file only in an approved location.
11

Resolve issues

Troubleshooting

The page stays on a skeleton loader +

Check your connection, then refresh once. If it continues, record the affected view and time and contact the administrator.

The Growth Performance graph is blank +

If the legend appears but no axes or lines are visible, refresh. If axes are visible but lines are flat, confirm the date range contains saved metrics.

My changes are not visible +

Confirm the save completed, clear active filters, and verify the selected date range.

I cannot edit or delete a record +

Your role may not permit the action, or the workspace may be locked. Contact an administrator when access is required.

The workspace says it is offline +

Restore the connection and use Retry sync before continuing edits.

Still blocked?

Include the page, content ID, time, and a screenshot without sensitive information.
